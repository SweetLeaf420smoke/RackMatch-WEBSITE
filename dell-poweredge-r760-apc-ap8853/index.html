Why this cable

A R760 PSU in this catalog uses a C20 inlet. APC lists AP8853 as 36 × C13 and 6 × C19. Use a C19 outlet and a C19–C20 jumper. A C13–C14 cord does not fit this inlet. Lower-watt R760 PSUs can be C14 and then use a C13 outlet instead.

Frequently asked questions

What power cable connects the Dell PowerEdge R760 to the APC AP8853?

An IEC C19–C20 jumper. C19 into the R760 C20 inlet; C20 into an AP8853 C19 outlet.

What outlets does the APC AP8853 have?

The vendor lists 36 × C13 and 6 × C19, input 230 V, 32 A input.

Does the Dell PowerEdge R760 need locking power cords?

Not required for a standard install. Locking option on this PDU: Optional locking C13 (AP870x / similar); this pair uses C19.

Should both server PSUs go into the same PDU?

No. Use two PDUs on separate circuits (A and B feeds), one PSU in each, and size each PDU for the full load.
