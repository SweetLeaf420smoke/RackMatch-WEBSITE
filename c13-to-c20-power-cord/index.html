C13 to C20 power cord

This cord has a C13 coupler on one end and a C20 plug on the other. It connects equipment with a normal C14 inlet to a C19 outlet.

C13 endGoes into the C14 inlet on the server, switch or storage PSU
C20 endGoes into a C19 outlet on a rack PDU or UPS
RatingLimited by the C13 end: 10 A (IEC) or 15 A (North America)

When it is useful

  • All C13 outlets on the PDU are taken, but C19 outlets are free.
  • A UPS has C19 outlets and you want to plug in a server with a C14 inlet.
  • You are mixing high-watt GPU servers (C20) and normal 1U/2U servers (C14) on the same PDU and want to use every outlet.

What to check

The C19 outlet is usually on a 16 A or 20 A branch, and the C13 end of this cord is rated lower. That is fine for a PSU that draws within the C13 rating (most PSUs up to about 1600 W on 200–240 V). Do not use it to daisy-chain power strips. Check the PDU's per-bank breaker so the total load on that bank stays under its limit.

The reverse case

A C19-to-C14 cord (C20-inlet server into a C13 outlet) exists, but a high-watt PSU can draw more than a C13 outlet is rated for. Avoid it unless the PSU is power-capped. See C13 PDU, C19 server.
