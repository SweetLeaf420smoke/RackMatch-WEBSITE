C13 vs C19

By the RackMatch team · Updated 2026-11-10 · Sources linked on the page

Both are IEC 60320 couplers (the female end of a cord, or an outlet on a PDU). They are different sizes and do not fit each other's inlets.

How to tell which one you need

Look at the inlet on the power supply, not the server model. A C14 inlet takes a C13 cord. A C20 inlet takes a C19 cord. The same chassis can ship with either, depending on the PSU wattage. Example: Dell PowerEdge R740 uses C14 on 495–1600 W PSUs and C20 on the 2000 W and 2400 W PSUs (details).

Can I plug a C13 into a C19 outlet?

No, the shapes are different. To feed a C14-inlet server from a C19 outlet you use a C13-to-C20 cord: C13 into the server, C20 into the C19 outlet. See C13 to C20 power cord. The other way round (C20-inlet server, PDU with only C13 outlets) does not work: one C13 outlet cannot safely feed a 16 A PSU. See C13 PDU, C19 server.

Power per connector

At 230 V a C13 connection carries up to 2.3 kW (10 A) and a C19 up to 3.7 kW (16 A). On 208 V North American circuits the UL ratings give about 3.1 kW for C13 (15 A) and 4.2 kW for C19 (20 A). A PSU above roughly 1600–2000 W is usually built with a C20 inlet for this reason. Full table: power connector guide.

Cord wire size

C13 cords typically use 0.75–1.0 mm² conductors (18–14 AWG in North America). C19 cords use 1.5 mm² (14 or 12 AWG). The thicker cable of a C19 cord is a quick way to tell them apart in a bin of spares.

Which PDU outlet mix to buy

Count PSUs, not servers. For each PDU you need one C13 outlet per C14-inlet PSU and one C19 outlet per C20-inlet PSU on that feed. Common rack PDUs offer around 20–40 C13 and 3–12 C19. GPU racks often need all-C19 PDUs. The PDU calculator checks the power side.

Common mistakes

  1. Ordering cords by server model instead of PSU. The same chassis can use both.
  2. Using a C13-to-C20 cord as if it were a 16 A cord. It stays a 10 A cord.
  3. Buying C19 adapters to fit a C20 PSU on a C13 outlet. Not safe at full load.
  4. Running out of C19 outlets on a PDU that has plenty of C13s.
Ratings vary by cord and country. Check the cord's own marking and the PSU nameplate.

Frequently asked questions

Is C19 bigger than C13?

Yes. C19 is wider, with three flat pins in a row, and rated 16 A IEC (20 A UL). C13 is rated 10 A IEC (15 A UL).

Can I plug a C13 into a C19 outlet?

No. Use a C13 to C20 cord: C13 into the server, C20 into the C19 outlet.

How do I know if my server needs C13 or C19?

Look at the PSU inlet. A C14 inlet takes a C13 cord; a C20 inlet takes a C19 cord.
