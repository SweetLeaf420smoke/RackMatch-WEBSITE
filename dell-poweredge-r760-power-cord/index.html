Dell PowerEdge R760 power cord

By the RackMatch team · Updated 2026-11-10 · Sources linked on the page

Dell’s R760 shop power-cord list includes jumper cords C13/C14 at 0.6 m, 2 m and 4 m, 250 V, 10 A. That is the cord for a C14 inlet.

A 2400 W R760 PSU uses a C20 inlet (200–240 V / 16 A). Then the jumper is IEC C19–C20 into a PDU C19 outlet. See R760 + AP8853. A C13-only PDU does not work: R760 2400 W + PX3-5496V.

Do not put a 2400 W C20 inlet on a C13–C14 cord.

Frequently asked questions

What power cord does the Dell PowerEdge R760 need?

It depends on the PSU that is installed, not on the chassis. A PSU with a C14 inlet takes a C13 cord; a PSU with a C20 inlet takes a C19 cord. The table on this page lists which PSUs use which inlet.

Can I use a C13 cord on a C20 inlet?

No. C13 and C20 have different shapes and do not fit. A C20 inlet needs a C19 cord.

What cord goes from the Dell PowerEdge R760 to a rack PDU?

Usually a short jumper with the same connector family at both ends: C13 to C14 for a C13 PDU outlet, or C19 to C20 for a C19 outlet. A wall plug cord (NEMA 5-15, Schuko, BS 1363) is only for a wall socket.

Should both PSUs go to the same PDU?

No. For redundancy plug each PSU into a different PDU on a different circuit (A and B feeds). Size each PDU so it can carry the whole server load alone.
