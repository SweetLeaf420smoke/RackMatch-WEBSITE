HPE ProLiant DL380 Gen11 + APC AP8941 Power Cable

Compatible. Required cable: IEC C13–C14.

ConnectorC13 into the DL380 Gen11 C14 inlet; C14 into an AP8941 C13 outlet
Voltage / currentServer PSU 100–240 V / 10 A. PDU 200–208 V, 30 A input
LockingOptional locking C13 (AP870x / similar)

Why this cable

The AP8941 is not C19-only. Schneider lists 21 × C13 and 3 × C19. The DL380 Gen11 inlet in this catalog is C14, so use one of the C13 outlets and a C13–C14 jumper. High-watt Flex Slot kits can change the inlet; read the nameplate.
