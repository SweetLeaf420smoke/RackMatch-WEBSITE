HPE ProLiant DL380 Gen11 + APC AP8941 Power Cable

Compatible. Required cable: IEC C13–C14.

ConnectorC13 into the DL380 Gen11 C14 inlet; C14 into an AP8941 C13 outlet
Voltage / currentServer PSU 100–240 V / 10 A. PDU 200–208 V, 30 A input
LockingOptional locking C13 (AP870x / similar) on C13; locking C19 if specified

Why this cable

This catalog lists the DL380 Gen11 inlet as IEC C14. APC lists AP8941 as 21 × C13 and 3 × C19. For this inlet, plug into a C13 outlet with a C13–C14 jumper. Do not use a C19–C20 jumper on this PSU. C19 outlets are for a C20 inlet. High-watt Flex Slot kits can change the inlet; read the nameplate.
