Rules that prevent wrong orders

  1. The inlet on the installed PSU decides the cord, not the server model. Read the PSU label.
  2. Odd numbers are the female end (coupler/outlet), even numbers the male end (inlet/plug): C13 goes into C14, C19 into C20.
  3. A C13 cord does not fit a C20 inlet. A C19 cord does not fit a C14 inlet.
  4. Cord current rating is set by its weakest end. A C13-to-C20 cord is still a 10 A cord.
  5. North America: load circuits to 80% of the breaker for continuous load (30 A circuit = 24 A usable).
  6. A/B feeds: plug each PSU into a different PDU on a different circuit. One PDU must carry the whole load alone.
  7. High-watt PSUs often give full power only on 200-240 V. On 120 V they are derated.
