IEC 60320 equipment connectors

These are the cords between equipment and the PDU or wall cord. The pair names always go odd (female) to even (male).

Rules that prevent wrong orders

  1. The inlet on the installed PSU decides the cord, not the server model. Read the PSU label.
  2. Odd numbers are the female end (coupler/outlet), even numbers the male end (inlet/plug): C13 goes into C14, C19 into C20.
  3. A C13 cord does not fit a C20 inlet. A C19 cord does not fit a C14 inlet.
  4. Cord current rating is set by its weakest end. A C13-to-C20 cord is still a 10 A cord.
  5. North America: load circuits to 80% of the breaker for continuous load (30 A circuit = 24 A usable).
  6. A/B feeds: plug each PSU into a different PDU on a different circuit. One PDU must carry the whole load alone.
  7. High-watt PSUs often give full power only on 200-240 V. On 120 V they are derated.

Locking connectors

Locking C13 and C19 cords stop a cord from being pulled out by accident in a dense rack. Some lock with a latch on the cord, others need a PDU with locking outlets. Vendors use different systems, so check that the locking cord and the PDU outlet are made to work together. A normal cord still fits a locking outlet; it just does not lock.

Reading cord markings

  • IEC cords: a code like H05VV-F 3G1.0 means a harmonised flexible cord with 3 conductors of 1.0 mm². 16 A C19 cords use 1.5 mm².
  • UL cords: SJT 14AWG/3C means a 3-conductor 14 AWG jacketed cord. 20 A C19 cords normally use 12 AWG.
  • The connector body is stamped with its rating, for example 10 A 250 V or 16 A 250 V. The lowest rating on the cord is the cord's rating.
Ratings are typical values from the standards. Always check the marking on the cord and the PSU nameplate. Wiring of wall and PDU circuits is a job for a licensed electrician.

Frequently asked questions

Which numbers are male and which are female?

In IEC 60320 the odd number is the female end (coupler or outlet) and the even number is the male end (inlet or plug). C13 mates with C14, C19 with C20.

What is the most common rack connector?

C13/C14. Most PDU outlets are C13 and most 1U/2U server PSUs have C14 inlets.

What plug does a 208 V rack PDU use in the US?

Often NEMA L6-30 (30 A, 250 V, locking) for single-phase, or L21-30 for three-phase.

What plug does a 32 A PDU use in Europe or Africa?

Usually an IEC 60309 blue 2P+E 32 A plug for single-phase, or red 3P+N+E for three-phase.
