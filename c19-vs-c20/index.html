C19 vs C20

This is the larger IEC 60320 pair. It is the usual jumper for 16 A class inlets.

C20Inlet on a high-watt PSU
C19Connector on the jumper that plugs into that inlet
JumperIEC C19–C20. C19 into the server. C20 into a PDU C19 outlet
Current class16 A class. A C13–C14 jumper does not belong here

How to remember it

Same odd/even rule as C13/C14: C19 coupler into C20 inlet. NVIDIA DGX H100/H200 and B200 ship locking C19/C20 cords rated 250 VAC, 20 A, 1200 mm.
