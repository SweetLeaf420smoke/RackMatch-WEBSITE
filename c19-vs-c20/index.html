C19 vs C20

By the RackMatch team · Updated 2026-11-10 · Sources linked on the page

This is the larger IEC 60320 pair. It is the usual jumper for 16 A class inlets.

On this page
  1. Which end goes where
  2. When you need C13 instead
  3. How to remember it
  4. One server and one PDU
  5. Frequently asked questions

Which end goes where

Into the serverThe C19 coupler goes into the C20 inlet on the PSU
Into the PDUThe C20 plug goes into a C19 outlet on the PDU
C20Inlet on a high-watt PSU, and the plug end of the jumper
C19Coupler on the jumper, and the outlet on the PDU
Current class16 A class. A C13–C14 jumper does not mate with a C20 inlet

When you need C13 instead

If the inlet on the PSU is C14, the cord is a C13–C14 jumper, not this pair. See C13 vs C14.

How to remember it

Same odd/even rule as C13/C14: C19 coupler into C20 inlet. NVIDIA DGX H100/H200 and B200 ship locking C19/C20 cords rated 250 VAC, 20 A, 1200 mm.

One server and one PDU

Dell PowerEdge R760 with the 2400 W PSU in this catalog uses a C20 inlet. APC AP8941 has 21 C13 outlets and 3 C19 outlets. The jumper is C19–C20: C19 into the R760, C20 into one C19 outlet. A lower-watt R760 PSU can be C14 and then uses a C13 outlet instead.

Worked pair: R760 + AP8941.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between C19 and C20?

C19 is the female end (cord coupler or PDU outlet). C20 is the male end (PSU inlet or jumper plug).

What amp rating do C19 and C20 have?

16 A under IEC rules, usually 20 A under UL rules.

Where are C20 inlets used?

High-watt server PSUs, GPU servers, PDU inputs and UPS outputs.
