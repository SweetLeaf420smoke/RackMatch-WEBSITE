C13 or C19

Read the inlet on the installed PSU, not the chassis name. A C14 inlet takes a C13–C14 jumper: C13 into the server, C14 into a PDU C13 outlet. A C20 inlet takes a C19–C20 jumper: C19 into the server, C20 into a PDU C19 outlet.

What RackMatch checks

  • Server and PDU power inlet/outlet match (C13–C14, C19–C20) from the vendor-checked catalog
  • Unknown models and missing PSU data (never marked Verified)
  • PDU outlet type and outlet count vs cords on that PDU
  • Redundant PSUs landed on the same feed or the same PDU
  • 10 A vs 16 A jumper class when the pair is not a standard match
