Dell PowerEdge R650 + APC AP8853 Power Cable

Compatible. Required cable: IEC C13–C14.

ConnectorC13 into the R650 C14 inlet; C14 into an AP8853 C13 outlet
Voltage / currentServer PSU 100–240 V / 10 A. PDU 230 V, 32 A input
LockingOptional locking C13 (AP870x / similar)

Why this cable

The R650 PSU inlet is IEC C14. The AP8853 has C13 and C19 outlets. For this 10 A inlet, plug into a C13 outlet with a C13–C14 jumper. Do not use a C19–C20 jumper on this PSU.

Sources

Server: Dell PowerEdge R650 owner’s manual (PDF)

PDU: Schneider Electric APC AP8853
