Supermicro SYS-821GE-TNHR power cord

By the RackMatch team · Updated 2026-11-19 · Sources linked on the page

The SYS-821GE-TNHR is Supermicro's 8U server for NVIDIA HGX H100 or H200 8-GPU boards. Like other 8-GPU servers it needs many 16 A-class feeds.

PSUs6 × 3000 W Titanium as standard, optional 8 × 3000 W (4+4). Supermicro lists the 6-PSU setup as 4+2 or 3+3 depending on configuration
PSU partPWS-3K06G-2R, 54 V / 12 V output
Input per PSU200–207 V AC: 2880 W, 15.7–16 A. 207–240 V AC: 3000 W, 14.5–16 A
Inlet16 A class. The datasheet of the same 2U 3000 W Supermicro PSU family (PWS-3K02G-2R) lists an IEC 60320 C20 inlet; check the label on yours
Low lineNo 3000 W output below 200 V. Plan for 200–240 V

Which cord

With C20 inlets and C19 PDU outlets you need a C19-to-C20 jumper per PSU: C19 into the PSU, C20 into the PDU. Supermicro sells its own (for example CBL-PWCD-0376-IS, 3 ft). Six PSUs means six cords; the 8-PSU option means eight. Each PSU can draw about 16 A, so every cord and outlet must be 16 A / 20 A rated.

PDU sizing

Six or eight 3 kW PSUs means one server can need C19 outlets on more than one PDU per feed. Count C19 outlets first, because most rack PDUs have only 3–12 of them. Then check power with the PDU calculator using measured draw, not 6 × 3000 W.

Frequently asked questions

What power cord does the Supermicro SYS-821GE-TNHR need?

A 16 A class C19-to-C20 jumper per PSU, C19 into the PSU and C20 into a C19 PDU outlet. Check the inlet label on your PSUs.

How many power supplies does the SYS-821GE-TNHR have?

Six 3000 W Titanium PSUs as standard, with an optional eight-PSU (4+4) setup.

What voltage does it need?

200–240 V AC. Each PSU delivers 2880 W at 200–207 V and 3000 W at 207–240 V, drawing up to 16 A.
