Dell PowerEdge R760 + APC AP8941 Power Cable

Compatible for the 2400 W PSU. Required cable: IEC C19–C20.

ConnectorC19 into the R760 C20 inlet; C20 into an AP8941 C19 outlet
Voltage / currentServer PSU 200–240 V / 16 A. PDU 200–208 V, 30 A input
LockingLocking C19/C20 jumper if specified

Why this cable

Schneider lists the AP8941 as 21 × C13 and 3 × C19. A 2400 W R760 PSU has a C20 inlet, so use a C19 outlet and a C19–C20 jumper. A C13–C14 cord does not fit this inlet. Lower-watt R760 PSUs can be C14 and then use a C13 outlet instead.

Sources

Server: Dell PowerEdge R760 PSU specifications

PDU: Schneider Electric APC AP8941
