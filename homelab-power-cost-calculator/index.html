Ways to cut the bill

  • Idle draw matters most: a server idling at 120 W around the clock uses over 1,000 kWh a year.
  • Consolidate small boxes onto one efficient host, and spin down drives that are rarely used.
  • Power-limit GPUs (nvidia-smi -pl): inference usually loses little speed.
  • Old servers often idle at 2-3x the power of a modern mini PC doing the same job.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate the running cost of a server?

Watts × hours ÷ 1000 gives kWh. Multiply kWh by your price per kWh. A 100 W device running all month uses about 73 kWh.

Should I use idle or peak watts?

Use the average draw you measure with a power meter or the PDU. Peak watts overstate the bill.

What is the overhead percentage for?

It covers cooling, UPS losses and network gear that runs because of the servers.
