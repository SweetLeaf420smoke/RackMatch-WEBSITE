Ways to cut the bill

  • Idle draw matters most: a server idling at 120 W around the clock uses over 1,000 kWh a year.
  • Consolidate small boxes onto one efficient host, and spin down drives that are rarely used.
  • Power-limit GPUs (nvidia-smi -pl): inference usually loses little speed.
  • Old servers often idle at 2-3x the power of a modern mini PC doing the same job.
