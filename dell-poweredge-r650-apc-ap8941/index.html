Dell PowerEdge R650 + APC AP8941 Power Cable

Compatible. Required cable: IEC C13–C20.

ConnectorC13 into the R650 C14 inlet; C20 into an AP8941 C19 outlet
Voltage / currentServer PSU 100–240 V / max 10 A on this jumper. PDU 230 V, 32 A input
LockingC19 outlets; use a locking C19/C20 jumper if specified

Why this cable

The R650 inlet is C14. The AP8941 has C19 outlets only, so the PDU end is C20. The C13/C14 side stays 10 A class. Do not treat this as a 16 A feed.

C13/C14 is 10 A class. Do not treat this as a 16 A feed.
