Dell PowerEdge R650 + APC AP8941 Power Cable

Compatible. Required cable: IEC C13–C14.

ConnectorC13 into the R650 C14 inlet; C14 into an AP8941 C13 outlet
Voltage / currentServer PSU 100–240 V / 10 A. PDU 200–208 V, 30 A input
LockingOptional locking C13 (AP870x / similar) on C13; locking C19 if specified

Why this cable

This catalog lists the R650 inlet as IEC C14. APC lists AP8941 as 21 × C13 and 3 × C19. For this inlet, plug into a C13 outlet with a C13–C14 jumper. Do not use a C19–C20 jumper on this PSU. C19 outlets are for a C20 inlet.

Sources

Server: Dell PowerEdge R650 owner’s manual (PDF)

PDU: Schneider Electric APC AP8941
