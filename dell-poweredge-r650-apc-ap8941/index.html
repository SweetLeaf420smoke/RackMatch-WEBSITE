Why this cable

This catalog lists the R650 inlet as IEC C14. APC lists AP8941 as 21 × C13 and 3 × C19. For this inlet, plug into a C13 outlet with a C13–C14 jumper. Do not use a C19–C20 jumper on this PSU. C19 outlets are for a C20 inlet.

Frequently asked questions

What power cable connects the Dell PowerEdge R650 to the APC AP8941?

An IEC C13–C14 jumper. C13 into the R650 C14 inlet; C14 into an AP8941 C13 outlet.

What outlets does the APC AP8941 have?

The vendor lists 21 × C13 and 3 × C19, input 200–208 V, 30 A input.

Does the Dell PowerEdge R650 need locking power cords?

Not required for a standard install. Locking option on this PDU: Optional locking C13 (AP870x / similar) on C13; locking C19 if specified.

Should both server PSUs go into the same PDU?

No. Use two PDUs on separate circuits (A and B feeds), one PSU in each, and size each PDU for the full load.
