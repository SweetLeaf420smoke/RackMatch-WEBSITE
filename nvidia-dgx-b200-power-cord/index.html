NVIDIA DGX B200 power cord

NVIDIA’s B200 table matches H100 on the cord itself: C19/C20, 250 VAC, 20 A, 1200 mm, six qualified locking cords in the box.

PDU side: push to insert, squeeze both clips to remove. PSU side: gray lock ring, padlock marks lock/unlock.
