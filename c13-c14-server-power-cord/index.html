C13 C14 server power cord

By the RackMatch team · Updated 2026-11-10 · Sources linked on the page

Required cable for a C14 inlet: IEC C13–C14 jumper. C13 into the server. C14 into the PDU C13 outlet.

What to order

Dell lists C13/C14 jumpers in 0.6 m, 2 m and 4 m on PowerEdge R760 power-cord options (250 V, 10 A class). HPE pre-configured ProLiant servers ship a 6-foot IEC C13/C14 jumper (A0K02A) with standard AC Flex Slot PSUs.

In a shallow rack with a Zero-U PDU, a 2 m or 4 m cord is extra slack. A short jumper (tens of centimetres) is the usual fix so the cord does not block rails or the next chassis.

Related: C13 vs C14, Dell PowerEdge R760 power cord.

Sources: Dell R760 shop power-cord list; HPE DL380 Gen10 / Gen10 Plus QuickSpecs (A0K02A).

Frequently asked questions

What is a C13 to C14 cord?

A jumper with a female C13 coupler on one end and a male C14 plug on the other. The C13 end goes into the server PSU, the C14 end into a C13 outlet on the PDU.

How many amps is a C13 to C14 cord?

10 A under IEC rules, and usually 15 A under UL rules in North America. Check the marking on the cord.

Is a C13 to C14 cord the same as a computer extension cable?

Yes. It is often sold as a monitor or PC extension cable. For racks, buy the length you need, usually 0.6 to 2 m.
