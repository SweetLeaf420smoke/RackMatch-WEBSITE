NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 rack power

By the RackMatch team · Updated 2026-11-19 · Sources linked on the page

A GB200 NVL72 rack is not cabled like a normal rack. There are no server power cords and no C13/C19 PDUs for the compute trays. Power comes in through power shelves and is spread through a DC bus bar at the back of the rack.

Rack powerAbout 120 kW (NVIDIA); partners quote 125–135 kW
Power shelves1U shelves rated 33 kW each, with six 5.5 kW PSUs per shelf
Shelf countEight shelves with N+N redundancy in NVIDIA's DGX GB200 design (Supermicro: 4+4, 132 kW total)
DistributionShelves convert AC to about 50 V DC and feed a vertical bus bar; trays blind-mate onto it
Shelf inputThree-phase AC, for example 346–480 V line-to-line (200–277 V line-to-neutral), via power whips from a remote power panel
CoolingDirect liquid cooling; the rack needs well over 100 kW of heat removal

What this means for cabling

  • No jumpers to the GPU trays. Compute and NVLink switch trays draw power from the bus bar.
  • Big three-phase feeds. Each power shelf gets its own high-current three-phase whip from a power panel or busway, not from a rack PDU.
  • Normal cords still exist for the management switch, top-of-rack network gear and in-rack CDU pumps. Those use regular C13/C19 cords from a small PDU.
  • Most data halls are not ready. Typical colocation racks are built for 10–15 kW; one NVL72 rack needs about ten times that.

Frequently asked questions

How much power does a GB200 NVL72 rack use?

About 120 kW according to NVIDIA; partners quote 125–135 kW depending on configuration.

Does GB200 NVL72 use C19 power cords?

Not for the compute trays. Power shelves convert three-phase AC to about 50 V DC and feed a bus bar that the trays plug into. Regular cords are only used for small items like management switches.

How many power shelves are in a GB200 NVL72 rack?

NVIDIA's DGX GB200 design uses eight 33 kW shelves with six 5.5 kW PSUs each, in N+N redundancy.

Can a normal colocation rack host a GB200 NVL72?

Usually not. Typical racks are built for 10–15 kW, while an NVL72 needs about 120 kW of power and liquid cooling.
