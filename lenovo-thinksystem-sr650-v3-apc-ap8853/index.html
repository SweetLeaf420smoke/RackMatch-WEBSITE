Lenovo ThinkSystem SR650 V3 + APC AP8853 Power Cable

Compatible. Required cable: IEC C13–C14.

ConnectorC13 into the SR650 V3 C14 inlet; C14 into an AP8853 C13 outlet
Voltage / currentServer PSU 100–240 V / 10 A. PDU 230 V, 32 A input
LockingOptional locking C13 (AP870x / similar)

Why this cable

The SR650 V3 inlet in this catalog is C14. Plug the AP8853 C13 outlet with a C13–C14 jumper. High-watt Lenovo PSU SKUs can use a larger inlet; check the PSU you installed.
