Dell PowerEdge XE9680 power cord

By the RackMatch team · Updated 2026-11-19 · Sources linked on the page

The XE9680 is Dell's 6U, 8-GPU server. Its power is very different from a normal 2U server: six high-watt PSUs with a high-temperature C22 inlet, and up to about 11.5 kW per server.

PSUs6 × 2800 W Titanium (200–240 V AC or 240 V DC); a 3200 W 277 V AC / HVDC option exists for some GPU builds
PSU inletIEC 60320 C22 (high-temperature version of C20)
Max draw with H100 GPUsAbout 11.5 kW per server (Dell rack integration guide)
Redundancy3+3. In 3+3 mode Dell notes the system may throttle if a PSU fails, to protect the remaining PSUs
Low line (100–120 V)Not supported. Plan for 200–240 V
Dell PDU recommendationC19 receptacles, one circuit breaker per power supply

Which cord

Dell recommends C19 outlets on the PDU, and the PSU inlet is C22. The cord that joins them is a C21-to-C20 jumper: the C21 end goes into the PSU's C22 inlet, the C20 end into the PDU's C19 outlet. A plain C19 cord does not fit a C22 inlet, because the C22 has a notch that only the high-temperature C21 passes. You need six of these cords per server.

How many PDUs and circuits

Six PSUs at up to 11.5 kW per server is far above what a typical 5 kW (208 V, 30 A) PDU can carry. Split the six PSUs across at least two PDUs on separate feeds (three per side), and size each side for the full server load if you want to survive a feed failure. Many XE9680 racks hold only 2–4 servers because of power, not space. Use the PDU calculator with your measured draw.

Frequently asked questions

What power cord does the Dell PowerEdge XE9680 use?

The PSUs have C22 inlets. With the C19 PDU outlets Dell recommends, use a C21-to-C20 jumper: C21 into the PSU, C20 into the PDU. You need six per server.

How much power does an XE9680 draw?

Dell gives an approximate maximum of 11.5 kW with H100 GPUs. Plan PDUs and circuits from that number, not from 6 × 2800 W.

Can the XE9680 run on 120 V?

No. The 2800 W PSUs need 200–240 V.

Will a normal C19 cord fit the XE9680?

No. A C19 does not fit a C22 inlet. You need the high-temperature C21 connector on the server end.
