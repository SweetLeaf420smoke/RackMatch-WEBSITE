HPE ProLiant DL380 Gen11 power cord

Required cable for the C14 inlet used in our catalog: IEC C13–C14. 100–240 V / 10 A class on that jumper.

Worked PDU pages: DL380 Gen11 + AP8853 (C13–C14), DL380 Gen11 + AP8941 (C13–C20 onto C19, still 10 A on C13/C14).
