C13 to C14 compatibility

By the RackMatch team · Updated 2026-11-10 · Sources linked on the page

Yes: C13 is made to mate with C14. That is the standard 10 A jumper used on most 1U/2U PSUs.

No: C13 does not mate with C20. If the inlet is C20, you need C19, not C13.

No: plugging C13 into a C14 inlet does not turn the circuit into 16 A. Stay at the nameplate current (often 10 A on Dell C13/C14 jumpers).

Related: C13 vs C14, C13 C14 server power cord.

IEC 60320 sheet: C13 coupler, C14 inlet.

Frequently asked questions

Does C13 fit C14?

Yes. C13 is the female coupler and C14 is the male inlet or plug. They are made to mate.

Does C13 fit C16?

No. C16 has a notch that blocks C13. Use a C15 cord for a C16 inlet. A C15 cord also fits a C14 inlet.

Does C13 fit C20?

No. C20 is a larger 16 A inlet and needs a C19 cord.
