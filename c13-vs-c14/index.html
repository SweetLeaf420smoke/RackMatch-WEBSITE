C13 vs C14

They are not two names for the same plug. They are the two ends of the common 10 A IEC 60320 pair.

C14Inlet on the server PSU (or the PDU outlet face that accepts C14)
C13Connector on the jumper that plugs into the PSU inlet
JumperIEC C13–C14. C13 into the server. C14 into a PDU C13 outlet
Current class10 A class. Not a 16 A C19/C20 feed

How to remember it

The number on the inlet is even (C14, C20). The number on the cord coupler is odd (C13, C19). A C13 cord goes into a C14 inlet.

Related: C13 to C14 compatibility, C13 C14 server power cord.

Check the live nameplate. High-watt PSUs on the same chassis can use C20 instead of C14.
