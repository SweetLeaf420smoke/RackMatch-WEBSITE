C13 vs C14

They are not two names for the same plug. They are the two ends of the common 10 A IEC 60320 pair.

Which end goes where

Into the serverThe C13 coupler goes into the C14 inlet on the PSU
Into the PDUThe C14 plug goes into a C13 outlet on the PDU
C14Inlet on the server PSU, and the plug end of the jumper
C13Coupler on the jumper, and the outlet on the PDU
Current class10 A class. Not a 16 A C19/C20 feed

When you need C19 instead

Look at the inlet on the PSU, not at the chassis name. If that inlet is C20, the cord is a C19–C20 jumper: C19 into the server, C20 into a PDU C19 outlet. A C13–C14 cord does not mate with a C20 inlet. See C19 vs C20.

How to remember it

The number on the inlet is even (C14, C20). The number on the cord coupler is odd (C13, C19). A C13 cord goes into a C14 inlet.

One server and one PDU

Dell PowerEdge R650, common PSU inlet C14, with APC AP8941. Schneider lists that PDU as 21 C13 outlets and 3 C19 outlets. The jumper is C13–C14: C13 into the R650, C14 into one of the C13 outlets. Leave the C19 outlets for a PSU that has a C20 inlet.

Worked pair: R650 + AP8941.
