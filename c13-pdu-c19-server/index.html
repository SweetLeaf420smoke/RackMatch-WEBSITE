C13 PDU, C19 server

By the RackMatch team · Updated 2026-11-10 · Sources linked on the page

This mix is the common failure in a rack: the PDU is C13-only (Raritan PX3-5496V in our catalog: 24 × C13, no C19) and the PSU inlet is C20 (C19 coupler on the cord). Worked example: R760 2400 W + PX3-5496V.

C13 will not plug into C20. Even with an adapter, the C13 path is 10 A class and the inlet is 16 A class. That is not a valid feed.

Fix: a PDU with C19 outlets and an IEC C19–C20 jumper. Example on this site: Dell R760 2400 W + APC AP8853 uses C19 on the PDU, not C13.

Related: PDU power cord compatibility, R760 + AP8853.

Do not use a cheater adapter to force C13 onto a C20 inlet.

Frequently asked questions

Can I power a C20-inlet server from a C13 PDU outlet?

No. The C13 outlet is 10 A class and the C20 inlet is 16 A class. Adapters that do this are not safe for a full-load PSU.

What should I do instead?

Use a PDU with C19 outlets and a C19 to C20 jumper, or choose a lower-wattage PSU option for the server that has a C14 inlet.

Why do some PSUs have C20 inlets?

High-watt PSUs, usually about 2000 W and up, draw more current than a C13/C14 connection is rated for.
