Dell PowerEdge R650 + Eaton EMA333-10 Power Cable

Compatible. Required cable: IEC C13–C14.

ConnectorC13 into the R650 C14 inlet; C14 into an EMA333-10 C13 outlet
Voltage / currentServer PSU 100–240 V / 10 A. PDU 120/208 V, 24 A input
LockingC13 and C19 outlet grip

Why this cable

This catalog lists the R650 inlet as IEC C14. Eaton lists EMA333-10 as 21 × C13, 6 × C19 and 1 × 5-20R. For this inlet, plug into a C13 outlet with a C13–C14 jumper. Do not use a C19–C20 jumper on this PSU. C19 outlets are for a C20 inlet.

Server: Dell PowerEdge R650 owner’s manual (PDF)

PDU: Eaton EMA333-10
