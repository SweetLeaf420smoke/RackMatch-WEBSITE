NVIDIA DGX H100 power cord

NVIDIA documents one qualified cord for DGX H100 (same table as H200): locking C19/C20, 250 VAC, 20 A, 1200 mm. The system ships with six of those cords.

PlugIEC 60320 C19/C20
Electrical250 VAC, 20 A
Length1200 mm
PDU endPush in. Remove by pressing both side clips
PSU endTwist lock ring to lock

Related: DGX H100 PDU requirements, DGX H200 power cord.

NVIDIA DGX H100/H200 User Guide: locking power cord specification
