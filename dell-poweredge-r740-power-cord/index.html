Dell PowerEdge R740 power cord

The cord depends on which PSU is installed. Check the wattage on the PSU label.

PSUInletCordNote
495 W, 750 W, 1100 WC14C13–C14 to a PDU C13 outlet1100 W gives 1050 W on 100–120 V
1600 WC14C13–C14Only 800 W on 100–120 V
2000 WC20C19–C20 to a PDU C19 outletDell kit note: C19/C20 cord required; 1000 W on 100–120 V
2400 WC20C19–C20200–240 V only

The same applies to the R740xd. Both PSUs in one server must be the same wattage.

Which PDU outlets

With 495–1600 W PSUs, any C13 outlet works, and a standard Dell C13–C14 PDU-style jumper (for example 450-ABLE, 0.6 m, 10 A) fits. With 2000 or 2400 W PSUs you need C19 outlets, and most PDUs only have a few. Plan them before ordering.
