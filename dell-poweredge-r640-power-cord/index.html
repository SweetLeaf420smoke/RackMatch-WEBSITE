Dell PowerEdge R640 power cord

Common R640 AC PSUs have a C14 inlet. The cord is a C13–C14 jumper: C13 into the server, C14 into a C13 outlet on the PDU.

PSUInletOn 100–120 V
495 WC14Full rating
750 WC14Full rating
1100 WC14Derated to 1050 W
1600 WC14Derated to 800 W

Dell's R640 technical guide notes that PSUs rated 1600 W and higher need 200–240 V to deliver their full capacity. On a 120 V homelab circuit, a 1600 W PSU behaves like an 800 W one.

Which cord to buy

A C13–C14 PDU-style jumper, 10 A, such as Dell 450-ABLE (0.6 m) or 450-ABLC (2 m). For a wall outlet instead of a PDU, use a country cord with a C13 end (for example NEMA 5-15P to C13 in North America).
