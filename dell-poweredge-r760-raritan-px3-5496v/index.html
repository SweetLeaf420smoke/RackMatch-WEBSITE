Dell PowerEdge R760 + Raritan PX3-5496V Power Cable

Compatible for the 2400 W PSU. Required cable: IEC C19–C20.

ConnectorC19 into the R760 C20 inlet; C20 into a PX3-5496V C19 outlet
Voltage / currentServer PSU 200–240 V / 16 A. PDU 230 V, 32 A input
LockingSecureLock compatible C13/C19

Why this cable

The PX3-5496V has C13 and C19. The 2400 W R760 inlet is C20, so use a C19 outlet and a C19–C20 jumper. Do not plug this PSU into C13.

Sources

Server: Dell PowerEdge R760 power supply units

PDU: Raritan PX3

Check the live nameplate and the vendor PDF before you order cable. High-watt PSUs on the same chassis can change the inlet.
