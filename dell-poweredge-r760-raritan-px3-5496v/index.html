Dell PowerEdge R760 + Raritan PX3-5496V Power Cable

Not compatible for the 2400 W PSU. No standard jumper.

ConnectorServer C20 inlet needs a C19 outlet on the PDU. PX3-5496V has C13 only
Voltage / currentServer PSU 200–240 V / 16 A vs PDU C13-only, 208 V, 30 A (24 A rated)
Lockingn/a

Why there is no match

Raritan’s PX3-5496V spec is 24 × IEC C13. There is no C19. A 2400 W R760 PSU uses a C20 inlet. C13 will not plug into C20, and the C13 path is 10 A class against a 16 A class inlet.

Fix: a PDU with C19 outlets and an IEC C19–C20 jumper. Example on this site: R760 + AP8853. See also C13 PDU, C19 server.

C13 outlet is 10 A class. This PSU inlet is 16 A class. Do not use a cheater adapter.

Check the live nameplate and the vendor PDF before you order cable. High-watt PSUs on the same chassis can change the inlet.
