Dell PowerEdge R650 + Raritan PX3-5496V Power Cable

Compatible. Required cable: IEC C13–C14.

ConnectorC13 into the R650 C14 inlet; C14 into a PX3-5496V C13 outlet
Voltage / currentServer PSU 100–240 V / 10 A. PDU 208 V, 30 A (24 A rated)
LockingSecureLock ready on C13

Why this cable

Raritan’s PX3-5496V spec is 24 × IEC C13. There is no C19 on this SKU. The R650 inlet here is C14, so a C13–C14 jumper is the match. A C19–C20 cord has nowhere to land on this PDU.
